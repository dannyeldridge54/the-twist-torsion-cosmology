## 4. Filter rule (how the headline is chosen)

- The headline goes to the lowest-ΔAIC model whose verdict is solid. If the Late Twist is solid and at least AS GOOD AS w0–wa, it leads, and the shared-F test (PREREGISTRATION_SHARED_F.md) follows.
