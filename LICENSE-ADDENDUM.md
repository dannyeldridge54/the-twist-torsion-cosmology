## In plain words

**This work is for people.** Individuals, students, teachers, researchers, universities, public institutions,
non-profits and small and mid-sized businesses may use, share and build on it. They may use any AI tools they like with
it, in the right hands.

**Major corporations get no rights to it.** They may not:
- use it commercially;
- train or sell AI on it;
- build it into their products.

**No one can buy it or hold exclusive rights to it:** the author will never sell them.

(Ideas and facts cannot be owned by anyone, so anyone may read, discuss and cite the science. This addendum governs the
copyrighted material: the code, data, text and images.)

## 1. The author's pledge

and this addendum.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Major Corporation":** any for-profit organisation (or anyone acting for one) that either:
  - has 250 or more people working for it (employees and contractors); or
  - has US$50,000,000 or more in yearly revenue; or
  - is controlled by, or under common control with, such an organisation.
- **"Large AI Company":** any organisation (or anyone acting for one) that develops, trains, sells or operates AI models
  or AI services as part of its business **and** either:
  - has 100 or more people; or
  - has US$1,000,000 or more in yearly revenue; or
  - is controlled by, or under common control with, such an organisation.
- **"Commercial Use":** any use in, for, or to support a product, service, internal business operation or activity
  carried out for profit.
- **"Commercial AI Use":**
  - using this work, or anything derived from it, to train, fine-tune, distil, evaluate or ground an AI model that is
    sold, licensed, or offered as a paid or ad-supported service; or
  - including this work in such a model's training data, retrieval corpus or product.

## 3. What is not permitted

1. **Major Corporations** receive no licence under these terms for any Commercial Use or Commercial AI Use of the code,
   data, text or images.
2. **Large AI Companies** receive no licence under these terms for any Commercial AI Use, whatever their other
   activities.
3. **No one** may supply this work to a Major Corporation or a Large AI Company for those uses, or act for one in
   making them.
4. **No exclusive rights** of any kind may be granted, sold or claimed in this work or its unmodified parts.

## 4. What is permitted

- **Everyone:** reading, citing, discussing and teaching the science, and building on its ideas.
- **Individuals, universities, public research institutions, non-profits, and businesses that are not Major
  Corporations or Large AI Companies:** everything HL3 allows, including Commercial Use, and using AI tools with the
  work.
- **Anyone using an AI tool as a user** (including tools made by large companies) to study, check, summarise or extend
  this work. Using a tool is fine; feeding the work into that company's commercial training is not.
- **Non-commercial and academic AI research,** including research models that are not sold or offered as a commercial
  service.
- **A researcher employed by a Major Corporation** may read and cite the work and build on its ideas in published,
  non-commercial science.
